Manage Activation

Last updated November 1, 2026

Brim license keys work offline, so there's no device count to manage and nothing to sign in to.

Move Brim to a new Mac

  • On the old Mac: ⋯ menu → License… → Deactivate on This Mac.
  • On the new Mac: download Brim, then ⋯ menu → Enter License… and paste your key.

See your license

⋯ menu → License… shows who the license belongs to. The Settings page shows it too, under the version number.

Can't find your key? Recover it here.
