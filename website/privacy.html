Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Short version: Brim doesn't collect anything about you.

The app

  • No analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
  • Trackpad touches are processed in memory, on your Mac, and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your license key is checked offline on your Mac.
  • The only network request is the update check: Brim downloads appcast.json from this website. It sends nothing but a standard request with the app version in the User-Agent.

Purchases

Payments are handled by our checkout provider, who acts as merchant of record and processes your name, email and payment details under their own privacy policy. We receive your email and order details so we can send and recover your license.

This website

No cookies and no third-party trackers.
