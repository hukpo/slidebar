Privacy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Short version: Brim doesn't collect anything about you.

The app

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • Trackpad touches are handled in memory on your Mac. They are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • The only time Brim uses the internet is to check for its own updates, and only if updates are turned on. That check asks for one small file and sends nothing about you.

This website

No cookies and no third-party trackers.
