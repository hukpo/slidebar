Privacy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Slidebar doesn’t collect anything about you. Your touches are handled on your Mac and never leave it.

The app

  • There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.
  • Trackpad touches are read in memory to recognize edge gestures. They are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Slidebar goes online for one thing: checking for its own updates, and only while updates are turned on. The check asks for one small file and sends nothing about you.
  • The optional Accessibility permission lets Slidebar show the on-screen display and press keys for you. Slidebar doesn’t read your screen or what you type.

This website

The site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, as described in GitHub’s own privacy statement.

The source code is public, so you can check all of this yourself on GitHub.
