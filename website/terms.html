No warranty

Brim is provided as it is, without any warranty. It uses parts of macOS that Apple can change at any time. We do our best to keep it working with new versions of macOS, but we can't promise it always will.

Permissions

Brim asks for Accessibility access so it can press the volume, brightness and media keys for you. It reads finger positions on your trackpad only to recognize edge gestures, on your Mac.
